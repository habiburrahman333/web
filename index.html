Project 02 · Analog electronics

Analog Timer Circuit

A hands-on analog electronics project. An RC timing circuit and an LM393 comparator turn an analog input into a digital HIGH output for approximately one minute. That output signals a relay to turn on a 220 VAC electric fan.

  • RC timing circuit
  • LM393 comparator
  • Voltage divider
  • Analog-to-digital threshold switching
  • Relay control
  • Electrical connections & safety awareness

Illustration only: a simplified concept diagram, not the original hardware design or schematic. This is a student learning project and has not been certified or professionally safety-tested.

How it works

  • RC timing. Based on resistor-capacitor circuit concepts; an LM393 comparator IC generates a digital output from an analog input.
  • Threshold. A simple voltage divider is used as the threshold reference for the RC circuit input.
  • Output. A digital HIGH for approximately one minute.
  • Load control. The output is the signal for a relay that turns on a 220 VAC electric fan.
  • Connection safety. Cable lugs are used on the AC cable-to-relay connections to improve connection reliability and electrical safety.
